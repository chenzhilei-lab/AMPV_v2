\section*{Table S3. Compact-PSF inferential summary}
All 40 contamination-versus-clean comparisons had 95\% sample-identity bootstrap intervals above zero. The 16 clean-domain adjacent-reference-contrast comparisons were numerical-floor cases; all 32 contaminated-domain adjacent comparisons had intervals below zero. Across the 10 contaminated cells, A--E, B--E, and C--E were above zero in 10/10 cells for each comparison; A--B and A--C were below zero in 10/10; B--C included zero in 10/10. These results are task-family specific and do not establish a universal workflow ordering.

\section*{Scope}
The SEP experiment is an implementation-transfer test on synthetic compact-PSF scenes with known source position. It is not observational validation, does not establish SEP superiority, and does not extend the tested reliability domain beyond the stated grid.
